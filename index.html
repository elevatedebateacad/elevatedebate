About Us


We are a student-led organization dedicated to making policy debate more accesible, fun, and impactful for students of all backgrounds. Our teaching model focuses on clear explanations, free resources, and support from professionals. Debate helps students develop critical thinking, public speaking, and advocacy skills.


Why Debate?


Debate strengthens students' public speaking and critical thinking skills. Not to mention, debate looks great on a college application as admissions officers know that these students have the critical qualities they look for.

Policy Debate


The type of debate we teach is known as Policy or Cross-Examination (CX). It is highly regarded as one of the best forms of debate as it comprises of structured argumentation, research, public speaking, and more. It is a great place for a student to start debate and expand from there.

Research

Research is a vital component of Policy Debate. Debaters will research for evidence and strategies as preparation for their next tournament.

Evidence

That research leads debaters to find the reliable evidence they are looking for. That evidence supports their arguments in rounds.

Reasoning

Evidence isn't everything. To tie it all together, debaters use reasoning to convince the judge using the evidence and arguments already stated.
